# Letter Merger

Version 16.1.0.0. Requires Windows with .NET Framework 4.8. Desktop Microsoft Word is needed for the layout check and PDF export.

1. Keep the four folders next to `LetterMerger.exe`, and keep the whole folder near the top of a drive, such as `C:\LetterMerger`.
2. Put your CSV UTF-8 file in `Data` and the photos in `Photos`, keeping the relative paths listed in the CSV.
3. Use the blank card in `Templates`.
4. Open `LetterMerger.exe`, confirm the four column mappings, choose the rows, preview, and generate.
5. Review the letters and every report in the new batch folder under `Output` before anything is sent.

Keep real records and everything the program creates in storage your institution approves. The full [README](../README.md), [privacy guidance](../docs/PRIVACY.md), and [security notes](../SECURITY.md) are in the extracted folder.
